# Community conduct

Keep contributions respectful, relevant, and verifiable. Explain disagreements with evidence and focus review on the work. Welcome contributors with different experience levels and language backgrounds.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, impersonation, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's credentials, account data, conversations, or private prompts.

Maintainer [Harzva](https://github.com/Harzva) may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect contributors and keep the project usable. Enforcement should be proportionate and explained without exposing private reports.

For a public content problem, report the affected link and the issue without repeating harmful material. For sensitive reports, request a private channel from the maintainer profile. Security issues follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
